Good to know

A few answers before you start.

Every list and every trip is a little different. We're here to help with yours.

How do I place a grocery order?

Choose items from the market or send your shopping list to the team. Include quantities and your delivery location so we can confirm the details.

Do you deliver to my area?

Share your neighbourhood and a nearby landmark with the team. We'll check delivery or pickup availability for your location before your order.

How much will my order and delivery cost?

Food prices depend on the items and quantities you choose. Ask for current prices; the team will confirm the order total and delivery fee before you go ahead.

What if something on my list isn't available?

We'll discuss available options with you. Let us know which items can be substituted and which ones you'd rather leave out.

Can I request an errand or a keke without buying food?

Yes. Send your errand details, or share your pickup point and destination for a keke. The team will confirm availability and cost with you.
